# Yoshio Mikami, *The Development of Mathematics in China and Japan* (1913)

A complete transcription of the English text of Yoshio Mikami's book (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1913; *Abhandlungen zur
Geschichte der mathematischen Wissenschaften*, Heft XXX), page by page, with the figures cropped from the scan.

- **Reader:** [mikami1913_en.pdf](mikami1913_en.pdf), 427 pages, with a linked table of contents.
- **LaTeX source:** [source/mikami1913_en.tex](source/mikami1913_en.tex) with the images in `source/img/`.
  Compile with XeLaTeX (font: Noto Serif); run it twice for the table of contents.

## Contents

Part I, *The Chinese Mathematics*, chapters 1–21: from the earliest period, the *Chou-pei* and the *Chiu-chang
Suan-shu* to Ch'in Chiu-shao, Li Yeh, Yang Hui and Chu Shih-chieh, the Ming dynasty, the arrival of European
mathematics, and later work on equations, π and infinite series.

Part II, *The Japanese Mathematics*, chapters 22–47: a general view and chronology of Japanese mathematics, Seki's
determinants, values of π, spherical volume and surface, Kurushima, Ajima, Aida, Shiraishi, Sakabe-Kawai, the *yenri*,
magic squares, the catenary, Hagiwara, the skew surface, and a notice of historical studies. An index follows.

## The edition

Transcribed from a scan of the University of Michigan copy digitized by Google (366 PDF pages). Each page of the reader
starts with a grey line giving the PDF page and the printed page number, so passages can be checked against the scan.
Running heads, page numbers and the scanner's marks are left out of the text; readings that could not be decided are
marked [? …].

PDF pages 1–269 were AI-transcribed with OpenAI models in June–August 2026 and collated against the scan by the
transcribing model. PDF pages 270–366 were AI-transcribed by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) in October 2026 from the scan,
page image by page image, and compared word for word with the Internet Archive text of the same printing.
